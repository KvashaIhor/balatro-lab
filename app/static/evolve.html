Evolves the weights of the value bot, the strategy that plays every round of a plan and that the planner looks ahead with: what it pays for jokers that grow or make money, packs, vouchers, money and interest. Fitness is progress: blinds beaten plus the share of the failed blind's target, so ante = progress ÷ 3 + 1, and 24 means ante 8 is beaten.

    Each generation, every set of weights plays the same fresh random seeds (a quarter on the Plasma Deck) with the value bot alone, no search. The generation's best then plays a fixed benchmark of 48 seeds, the same in every generation and every run, so the benchmark line shows real progress. The planner keeps its weights until you pick a run's champion with "Use these weights in the planner": weights that make the bot better alone don't always make the planner's search better, so check with a thorough plan.
